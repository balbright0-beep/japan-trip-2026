Sunrise coffee + breakfast EAT
Sunrise is about 5:35 AM. Nothing in town opens before 7, so make the sunrise coffee at the Airbnb — stock up at Super Ronny on the first morning. Strictly optional — but worth it once or twice for a dawn surf.
Morning
Surf Playa Santa Teresa / Playa Hermosa SURF 📍
Surf or an activity in the morning when it's glassy, beach in the afternoon into sunset. On a rainy morning, flip it. Santa Teresa is a sand-and-reef beach break, best on a rising tide; watch for rips, urchins and Suck Rock. Boards rent for about $20–30 a day; a 90-minute lesson with gear is about $70–100.
🦜 The Nicoya Peninsula is one of the world's five "Blue Zones" — places where an unusually large share of people live past 100.
Sat or Sun
Cabo Blanco Nature Reserve HIKE 📍
Be there at 8 AM. The Swedish Trail (Sendero Sueco) climbs first, then runs to a wild beach — about 4 km each way, so allow 5–6 hours and leave the beach by about 2 PM. $13.56 per person. Bring plenty of water, snacks, repellent, sandals and a swimsuit. The last mile in is rough and the free lot has had break-ins — leave nothing in the car.
⚠ Open Wednesday–Sunday, 8 AM–4 PM; closed Monday and Tuesday. On this trip that means Saturday or Sunday.
🦜 Cabo Blanco became Costa Rica's first protected area in 1963, thanks to a Swedish-Danish couple, Nicolás Wessberg and Karen Mogensen, who campaigned to save it.
Any day
Montezuma waterfalls + beach day WATERFALL BEACH 📍
About 30–45 minutes away, on the Gulf side of the peninsula. Two ways in: the free riverbed trail to the lower falls (about 20 minutes each way, slippery, with ropes — skip it if the river is up), or the Sun Trails entrance to the upper falls (about ₡3,000; park at Butterfly Brewing; open 8:30 AM–5:30 PM). Allow at least 1½ hours, and wear shoes that can get wet.
⚠ Don't jump. People have died cliff-jumping at the middle falls.
Any day
Mal País fishing village + tide pools BEACH 📍
Just south of Santa Teresa. Turn right at Indigo Yoga onto the dirt road Calle Mar Azul, keep left past the houses and over a big bump into a palm-lined field, and park. Go at low tide — it gets rough as the tide comes in. Water shoes; watch for urchins.
Afternoon
Beach massage RELAX
Brad's call: wing it with the beach pop-ups. Therapists set up tables on and around the beach near the main breaks and beach clubs — walk-up, cash, and usually well under spa prices.
Backups: Massage & Physio — By Juan (near Super Ronny 2, 7 AM–8 PM, +506 7130 9033; reviews unverifiable) · Numú Wellness Center (4.9★, 30 TripAdvisor reviews) · Jungle Wellness (Salva — physiotherapy-focused, rave reviews).
5:20 PM
Sunset, Playa Santa Teresa BEACH 📍
Sunset is about 5:16 PM. Afternoon beach straight into it — Banana Beach and El Carmen are the classic sunset bars.

Highlands Day: Jungle Hikes + Waterfalls

Thursday, November 12
cascadas

Brad's brief: a big jungle-hike day, waterfalls, off the beaten path. All three stay as options — decide on the day by weather and legs. None booked.

Pick oneSensoria · Río Celeste · Rincón
Book aheadReservation or SINAC ticket
SleepBagaces
Option A
Sensoria — private reserve HIKE WATERFALL 📍
North slope of Rincón de la Vieja. The most off-the-beaten-path choice: the "Land of Senses" tour is a 7 km guided hike through private primary rainforest to the blue Buenos Aires waterfall and the Jícara and Pilón thermal pools, small groups only, with lunch. Arrive 9 AM, done about 3 PM. Reviewers put it about 1½ hours from Liberia, with a rough last stretch — Sensoria says 4WD is required (the Jimny qualifies).
⚠ Needs an advance reservation: sensoria.cr or WhatsApp +506 8955-4971. $165 per adult — $132 if booked online by Nov 8.
Option B
Tenorio Volcano / Río Celeste HIKE WATERFALL 📍
About 1½ hours: Route 1 toward Cañas, turn off at the Bijagua sign about 10 km before Cañas, through Bijagua, then 9 km east on a paved road. The most epic single waterfall: the trail (about 3 km in, roughly 3 hours there and back) passes the sky-blue Río Celeste falls and Los Teñideros, where the river turns blue. Busy at the falls viewpoint, quiet deeper in. No swimming inside the park — there's a free river spot by the bridge about 1 km past the entrance. Parking about ₡3,000.
⚠ Tickets online only at serviciosenlinea.sinac.go.cr ($13.56; 1,200 visitors a day). Entry 8 AM–2 PM, everyone out by 4 PM. The staircase down to the falls reopened only partway after 2025 storm damage — check its status when you book.
🦜 Nothing is dyed: where two clear rivers meet at Los Teñideros ("the dyers"), a change in acidity makes tiny aluminosilicate particles clump together, and they scatter sunlight so the river looks sky-blue.
Option C
Rincón de la Vieja — Las Pailas + La Cangreja HIKE WATERFALL 📍
About 1 hour, to the Las Pailas entrance via Curubandé (a private-farm road toll of about ₡800 each). Volcanic jungle: the Las Pailas loop (3 km, about 2 hours) passes fumaroles and mud pots; La Cangreja is 5 km each way to a turquoise pool (4–5 hours round trip, no swimming), with Escondida and Oropéndola falls nearby. Open Tuesday–Sunday 8 AM–3 PM — closed Mondays, so Thursday is fine.
⚠ Tickets online only ($16.95) — nothing is sold at the gate. The waterfall trails stop admitting hikers at noon, so be at the gate by 8 AM for La Cangreja.
🦜 The park is part of the Área de Conservación Guanacaste, a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1999. "Rincón de la Vieja" means "the old woman's corner", from a local legend.
Fallback
Llanos de Cortés + Río Negro hot springs EASY DAY 📍
The light option. Llanos de Cortés waterfall is about 20 minutes from Bagaces: open 8 AM–4 PM (last entry 3:30), $7, free guarded parking, no 4x4 needed. Then the Río Negro hot springs at Hacienda Guachipelín, near Las Pailas: 10 pools plus volcanic mud, $35, 90-minute visits starting hourly 9 AM–4 PM.

Driving + Getting Around

Costa Rica drives on the right.
Santa Teresa roads are rough and partly unpaved. Allow the full drive times, and don't rush the Nov 6 night drive.
It gets dark fast: sunrise ~5:35 AM, sunset ~5:16 PM. Plan the Wednesday drive for daylight.
Liberia → Santa Teresa: Route 21 through Nicoya to Paquera, Route 160 to Cóbano, Route 618 into Santa Teresa. Ignore Google's west-coast shortcut — it has river crossings.
Fuel: Liberia, Nicoya, and Cóbano (the only station near Santa Teresa).
Ferry option: Paquera ↔ Puntarenas with Naviera Tambor — 6:30, 9:00, 12:00, 15:00 and later; about 70 minutes; arrive 45 minutes early. Not faster to Bagaces.
Weather: 2026 is an El Niño year and the forecast for early-to-mid November is very dry in the North Pacific — any rain comes in short bursts.
Your license: a US license is valid for driving here for 3 months.
Waze is what most drivers in Costa Rica use. Download offline Google Maps as a backup.
Gas stations are full-service — tell the attendant "lleno, por favor" (fill it up).
The National counter is at the Hilton Garden Inn in front of LIR, open 8 AM–5 PM.
Liberia (LIR) is Daniel Oduber Quirós International Airport.
National parks sell tickets online only through SINAC. Rincón de la Vieja closes Mondays; Cabo Blanco closes Mondays and Tuesdays.
Leave nothing visible in the car at beaches and trailheads.
