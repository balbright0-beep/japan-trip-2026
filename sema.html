Getting Around the LVCC

Vegas Loop (free on campus) Paradise Bridge (walk) Concourse (walk)
Show hours: 9:00 AM–5:00 PM Tue, Wed, Thu.
Walking from the Fontainebleau: about 15 min — south on Las Vegas Blvd, then east on Convention Center Dr to West Hall (300 Convention Center Dr).
Vegas Loop is free between the five LVCC stations — West, Riviera (for Resorts World), Central, Central Plaza (for Encore) and South — about 2 min against a walk of up to 25. LVCC stations run 6:15 AM–8 PM. Use it for every West ↔ Central hop.
Loop from the hotel: the Fontainebleau station is on Level V1 (far lane of Rideshare Pickup, South Lobby escalators) — $7 a ride to any LVCC station.
Monorail: the LVCC stop sits above Desert Inn Rd at Paradise Rd, across the Silver Lot from the halls. $6.25 a ride; 6 AM–2 AM show week.
Taxis + rideshare: the show's hub is the West Hall porte-cochère on Convention Center Dr, across from the Marriott.
West ↔ North: the Skywalk / Paradise Bridge.
North ↔ South: the climate-controlled concourse.
Diamond Lot is beside West Hall. Silver Lot is by the Monorail station.
Daylight + weather: sunrise ~6:05 AM, sunset ~4:41 PM. Normal highs ~73°F, lows ~52°F.
Booth-number blocks: North 10000s · Central 20000s · South Lower 40000s · West 50000s · Silver Lot 80000s. Within a hall, work the numbers in order.
